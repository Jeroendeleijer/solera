Tax and fiscal

Spain taxes non-residents on ownership, on income, on wealth and on every transfer. Your own country is looking at the same house from the other side. We handle the Spanish side, and give your own adviser exactly the summary they need.

Selling

On a sale the buyer withholds 3% of the price for the Spanish tax authority, and the town hall taxes the increase in land value. Both can be calculated before you agree a price — and anything over-withheld can be reclaimed.

Inheriting and passing on

On death a Spanish house falls under Spanish inheritance tax whatever your nationality, and the estate is accepted before a Spanish notary. With a Spanish will and a choice of your national law, that becomes a matter of weeks rather than a year.
