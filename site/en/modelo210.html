Three steps. You only do the first.

This is not a self-service portal. You send us one document; the rest happens here, with a person signing it off before anything reaches the tax authority.

1

You send your IBI bill

It carries everything we need: the cadastral value, the referencia catastral and the municipality. A phone photo is enough. Not sure which piece of paper it is? Send what you have and we will find it.

Your effort: about two minutes
2

We calculate and file

Our software reads the bill, applies that tax year's rules and prepares the return. A tax specialist checks and signs it; then it goes over the tax authority's official batch channel, collected by direct debit from your own home-country account.

Your effort: none
3

Receipt and annual check-up

Within three working days the official AEAT receipt is in your portal, with a summary in English. The annual check-up comes with it: whatever else is going on with this property, on one page.

Within three working days

What this is

  • The return on notional income — the one every non-resident owner must file each year, even on an empty house.
  • One filing per co-owner. A house in two names is two filings, at €29.99 each.
  • A garage or storeroom with its own referencia catastral is a filing of its own.
  • Letting the property? The rental return comes on top, annually in April, with an expense annex.

What the calculator does not do

  • Rental income. That is calculated separately, with costs deductible for EU/EEA residents.
  • Capital gains on a sale, or reclaiming the 3% retention.
  • Check the list of revised municipalities — we do that by hand before filing.
  • Account for assessments from earlier years that are still outstanding.
