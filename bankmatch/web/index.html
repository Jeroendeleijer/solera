bankmatch

Match payments to the invoices that explain them.

Drop in a bank export and a pile of invoice PDFs. Every pairing is scored and every match says why, so your accountant can disagree with one row without distrusting the rest.

Nothing leaves this page. The files are read in your browser and the matching runs there too. No upload, no server, no account — you can disconnect from the network and it still works.
